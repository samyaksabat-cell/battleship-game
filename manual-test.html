Battleship Manual Test Guide

How to Test the Game

The game loads ES modules, so serve the folder over HTTP (e.g. npx serve .) and open index.html from there.

Then follow these steps:

Step 1: Ship Placement

- Pick an AI difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) in the setup controls

- Click "Randomize Placement" to auto-place ships

- Or manually click on the grid to place ships

- Right-click to rotate ship orientation

- Click "Start Game" when all ships are placed

Step 2: Player Turn

- Click on the "Enemy Waters" grid to attack

- Red X = hit, White circle = miss

- Watch your ship count in the score display

Step 3: AI Turn

- AI will automatically attack after your turn

- Easy: fires at a random un-fired cell

- Medium: hunt mode (random) until it hits, then target mode (adjacent cells)

- Hard: probability density - it fires where the remaining ships fit most often

- Watch your ships get hit on the "Your Fleet" grid

Step 3b: Show AI Reasoning (Hard only)

- Tick "Show AI reasoning" during a Hard game

- Your fleet grid is tinted with the Hard AI's probability map, refreshed each turn

- Hits, misses and your ships stay visible underneath; unticking clears the tint

- With Easy or Medium selected a note explains the heatmap is Hard-only

Step 4: Game Over & Analysis

- Game ends when all ships of one player are sunk

- The post-game analysis shows your targeting efficiency and your three biggest missed opportunities

- Click "Play Again" to restart (difficulty, AI state and shot history all reset)

Known Issues to Check

- Ships don't overlap when placed manually

- AI doesn't fire on the same cell twice

- Game correctly detects when all ships are sunk

- Play Again button resets the game properly
